Privacy notice

Neon Wings is a free, local-first browser game. It has no account, contact form, payment, advertising, profiling, or first-party analytics.

Game data

Settings and saved progress are stored in this browser. They are not intentionally transmitted to an application database. The game does not need your real name, email, or date of birth.

Hosting

Vercel delivers the files and may process ordinary request and security logs. Google-hosted fonts were removed so the game no longer contacts Google Fonts on first load.

Controls

The local-saves page explains stored items and lets you erase accessible site storage. No optional tracking is used, so an opt-in marketing banner would be misleading.
